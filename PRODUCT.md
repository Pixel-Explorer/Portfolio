# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary** — Recruiters and businesses hiring for design, film, creative direction, or consulting roles. They're skimming under time pressure and need to verify breadth + depth in under 60 seconds. Their job: decide whether to interview.

**Secondary** — Peers, internet, fellow creatives. They browse slowly, follow craft, and share. Their job: be inspired, remember the name.

## Product Purpose

A cinematic personal portfolio for Anirudh Venkatesan ("Pixel Explorer") that proves hiring one operator delivers a studio's output across the entire creative spectrum — film, photo, design, web3, animation, strategy. The site is the pitch. Success = landing the next consulting role.

Archive Mode (built) renders ~120 ledger moments (1991→2026) as a sculptural 3D cluster. Story Mode (planned) is a scroll-locked cinematic narrative of 11 life eras.

## Brand Personality

cinematic · sculptural · brutalist

The voice is specific and evidence-backed: real dates, real numbers, real artifacts. Never generic portfolio language. The visual is architectural, weighty, and intentional — a gallery installation, not a slideshow.

## Anti-references

- Generic portfolio language ("passionate", "innovative", "creative")
- Stock photography (every visual is his own or AI-generated to spec)
- Gradient text, glassmorphism as default, bouncy spring animations
- Cards-inside-cards, identical card grids, tiny uppercase eyebrow labels on every section
- SaaS-cream or dark-mode-by-default color choices
- Login walls, analytics popups, cookie banners, or any friction before the work

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence-backed, not claimed.** Every fact has a proof artifact (email screenshot, contract, video still). If evidence is missing, mark it tentative or omit it.
2. **Specificity over polish.** "14 Oct 2010" not "October 2010." "539K views" not "went viral." Numbers and dates are the texture.
3. **One operator, studio output.** Every visual decision must reinforce that this is a one-person creative studio, not an agency or a team.
4. **Recruiter-first, peer-second.** The surface must work in a 60-second skim. Depth rewards those who linger.
5. **Architectural weight, not digital lightness.** Motion is heavy and eased (600–900ms), never bouncy. Typography is editorial and brutalist. The 3D scene is a physical model you orbit, not a game you play.
6. **Density over sprawl.** Cards breathe but don't balloon — sheet titles cap at 28px, body at 13px. A single-entry card should show the full story, evidence gallery, and metadata without requiring vertical scroll more than 2× the viewport. If it needs more space, the sizing is wrong, not the container.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast minimum (body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1)
- Dark/light mode toggle with localStorage persistence (built)
- `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for all animations
- Keyboard navigation for UI controls
- No analytics, no login, no tracking
