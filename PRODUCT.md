# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- **Primary:** recruiters and businesses hiring for design / film / consulting. Context: skimming under time pressure; must see in under 60 seconds that hiring Anirudh = hiring a one-person studio across design / film / photo / web3 / animation / strategy. Job: skim, verify evidence, contact.
- **Secondary:** peers, fellow creatives, the internet. Job: story, craft, personality — the slow burn.

## Product Purpose

Cinematic personal portfolio for Anirudh Venkatesan ("Pixel Explorer"). The site IS the pitch: a sculptural 3D archive city of 89 documented, evidence-backed career moments (1991 → 2026), with editorial case studies, role/client explorers, and a photography gallery. Success = the next consulting role. Desktop is the cinematic showcase; mobile is a stripped-down list/folder reader behind a gate.

## Brand Personality

Brutalist editorial · cinematic · evidence-backed. Confident, specific, archival — a film-production ledger, not a marketing page. Numbers over adjectives ("539K views", "$15K grant", "72 days"). Real artifacts (Gmail screenshots, contracts, stills) over polished mockups.

## Anti-references

- Generic portfolio language ("passionate", "creative", "innovative"); motivational filler.
- Parallax for parallax's sake; bouncy spring animations; gradient text; glassmorphism; emoji.
- Stock photography — every visual is his or generated to his spec.
- Template SaaS landing patterns (hero-metric blocks, identical card grids).

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence-backed only.** Every claim on the surface has a proof artifact or a tentative flag. If evidence is missing, omit — never invent.
2. **The archive is a living model of him, not a chronological map.** Importance = position and height; roles = color; time = a window you drag.
3. **Specificity is the voice.** Exact dates, real counts, actual documents. One hook line per scene, no paragraphs.
4. **Desktop-cinematic first, mobile parity second.** Never over-optimize mobile at desktop's expense.
5. **Chrome is owned at the root.** One authoritative chrome generation (fluent.css `@layer force`); no per-view patches that fork the shell.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected (motion collapses to fades/instant).
- No WCAG conformance target declared; keep interactive contrast ≥ 4.5:1 where it doesn't fight the committed dark-archive palette.
- No login, analytics, popups, or cookie banners.
